TeleChat TV Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · App package: com.thiru.tvgram

This policy explains what information TeleChat TV ("the app", "we", "us") handles when you use it on Android TV, where that information goes, and the choices you have. TeleChat TV is an independent app built on the official Telegram API (TDLib). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Telegram FZ-LLC.

In short
  • We do not run our own servers, and we do not collect, sell or receive your messages, contacts or media.
  • Your Telegram data travels directly between your TV and Telegram's servers, encrypted by Telegram's protocol.
  • Watch history, favourites and settings are stored only on your TV.
  • The video player shows ads through Google's Interactive Media Ads (IMA) SDK, which collects device and advertising identifiers.
  • The app has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

1. Information handled by the app

1.1 Telegram account and content

You sign in by scanning a QR code with the Telegram app on your phone. If your account has two-step verification, you enter your Telegram password on the TV; it is sent directly to Telegram to complete sign-in and is never stored by the app.

Once you are signed in, the app uses TDLib to load your chats, channels, groups, messages, contacts list, profile names and photos, and the photos, videos, audio and files shared in your chats, so that it can display and play them. This data is exchanged directly between your device and Telegram's servers. It is never sent to us or to any other third party. Telegram's handling of this data is governed by the Telegram Privacy Policy.

When you sign in, TDLib sends Telegram basic session details so the session can be listed in your Telegram "Active sessions": the device model, Android version and app version.

The app does not access your TV's own contacts, location, microphone, camera or other files. Its only Android permission is internet access.

1.2 Data stored on your device

The following is stored in the app's private storage on your TV and is not uploaded anywhere:

DataPurpose
Telegram session, message database and media cache (managed by TDLib)Keep you signed in, load chats quickly and avoid re-downloading media
Watch history: video name, chat name, thumbnail and playback position"Continue watching" and resuming playback
Favourites (watch list)Videos you saved to watch later
App preferences, such as your default chat filterRemember your settings

App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it does not leave your TV through backups.

1.3 Advertising

Longer videos may include short video ads before, during or after playback. Ads are served by Google's Interactive Media Ads (IMA) SDK and Google Ad Manager. To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app ID), and
  • ad interactions, such as impressions, skips and completions.

The app does not share the content of your messages or videos with the ad provider. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android TV settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Device Preferences → About → Ads, depending on your device).

2. How information is protected

  • Communication with Telegram uses Telegram's MTProto encryption.
  • All other network traffic, including ad requests, is HTTPS only; unencrypted connections are blocked.
  • Local data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Screenshots and screen recording are blocked while the video player is open.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use these measures to protect your information.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. Data leaves your device only:

  • to Telegram, to provide the Telegram service you signed in to, and
  • to Google, through the IMA SDK, to serve ads as described above.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Sign out from the app's settings to end your Telegram session on this TV. This deletes the Telegram data cached by TDLib, your watch history and your app preferences.
  • Clear cache in the app's settings to delete downloaded media at any time.
  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, to remove all data the app stored on the TV, including favourites.
  • You can also end the session remotely from Telegram on another device: Settings → Devices.
  • Data held by Telegram (your account, messages and media) is controlled through Telegram. You can delete your Telegram account at my.telegram.org/delete.

Because we do not hold any of your data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

5. Children's privacy

TeleChat TV is not directed at children under 13, and you must meet Telegram's minimum age requirement to use a Telegram account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Since your account data is held by Telegram and your local data stays on your TV, you can exercise these rights through Telegram, through the deletion options above, or by contacting us with any questions.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact.telechat@gmail.com.
